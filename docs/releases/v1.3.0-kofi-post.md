**Title:** TrailReplay 1.3 is here: give every route a cover

## Post body

Your route has a story. Now you can give it a first frame that looks the part.

TrailReplay 1.3 adds a cover designer for video exports. Pick a starting layout, then move and resize the map, title, photos, and stats right on the canvas. Add more photos, crop or zoom them inside their frames, choose a typography style, and make the stats your own with editable labels, values, colors, and activity icons. The cover appears for one second at the start of the exported video, so it can also work as a clear first frame in an Instagram feed.

There is more in this release:

- Dynamic elevation highlights bring notable climbs and descents into the replay.
- Real Pace follows recorded GPX and FIT timing, including pauses, to keep the route and media in sync.
- Studio exports have smoother map timing and improved H.264 quality.
- You can bring in a Strava activity with a browser bookmarklet and place route landmarks by coordinates.

Try it at [trailreplay.com](https://trailreplay.com). If you make a cover you like, I'd love to see it. Your support on Ko-fi helps me keep improving TrailReplay. Thank you for being here!
