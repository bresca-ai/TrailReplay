## Post body

Your route has a story. TrailReplay 1.3 lets you give it a first frame that looks the part. Watch the new release video above.

The biggest addition is a cover designer for video exports. Choose from four starting layouts, then move and resize the map, title, photos and stats directly on the canvas. Drop in multiple photos, crop and zoom them, choose typography and colors, and customize each stat with your own label, value and activity icon. The cover appears for one second at the start of the exported video, making it useful as a clear opening frame in an Instagram feed.

Also new in 1.3:

- Dynamic elevation highlights bring notable climbs and descents into the replay.
- Real Pace follows GPX and FIT timestamps, including pauses, so the route, media and annotations stay in sync.
- Studio exports have smoother map timing and improved H.264 video quality.
- Import a Strava activity GPX with the browser bookmarklet.
- Add route landmarks by entering coordinates.
- More typography choices, clearer mobile annotations, and better progress feedback for large imports.

Try the new tools at [trailreplay.com](https://trailreplay.com). Full release notes: [TrailReplay v1.3.0](https://github.com/bresca-ai/TrailReplay/releases/tag/v1.3.0).
