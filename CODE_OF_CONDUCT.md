# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the TrailReplay community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, giving and accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community, and respecting differing viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/bresca-ai/TrailReplay/security/advisories/new) so it can be handled privately. Project maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise moderate contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
